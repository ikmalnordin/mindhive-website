What Your Team Learns

From clean data habits to repeatable Power Query workflows.

Each module is built around realistic Malaysian business files — monthly sales exports, reconciliations, and multi-sheet trackers.

Module 01

Clean Workbook Architecture

Structure raw data properly with Excel Tables, data validation rules, and formatting standards that prevent broken reports as teams grow.

Module 02

Modern Formulas & Lookups

Replace brittle nested formulas with XLOOKUP, INDEX/MATCH, SUMIFS, COUNTIFS, IFERROR, and dynamic array functions for faster reconciliations.

Module 03

Power Query Automation

Combine monthly CSVs, clean messy text or dates, and refresh multi-source reports in one click without writing VBA macros.

PivotTables & Management Dashboards

Build interactive PivotTables, slicers, and executive summary views that answer leadership questions cleanly every week.
