Buyer context for all Tier 1/2 pages: medium enterprises with a fast decision loop (a staff member finds the course, finance/the owner approves) — not large GLC procurement cycles. Copy and CTAs should match that speed (clear pricing posture, low-friction next step), not a lengthy RFP-style pitch.

## Tier 1 — Commercial, training (existing money pages)

Priority order confirmed by owner: **Excel > Data > AI > Consulting/deployment.** Reasoning: AI training search results are dominated by large global names (OpenAI, Microsoft, Coursera); Excel is a more winnable, buyer-intent long-tail space for a Malaysia-based provider.

|---|---|---|---|
| `excel-training.html` | excel training malaysia; corporate excel training | Commercial | **Highest — Phase 2 starts here** |

Known direct competitors to check content/keyword gaps against in Phase 2: **Excel Academy Malaysia**, **excel.my** (niche local Excel training). **Skillsoft** and **Udemy** are broad international e-learning platforms — not a direct content-gap comparison, but worth noting in copy why Mindhive differs (Malaysia-based, onsite/live delivery, HRD Corp claimable, built on real data & AI delivery work, not generic video courses). **CAD Training** is a broader Malaysian corporate training provider, relevant to the `training.html` hub positioning.
