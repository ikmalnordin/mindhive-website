Common Questions

What HR & team leads usually ask.

Is Mindhive's corporate training HRD Corp claimable?
Yes — our corporate workshops are HRD Corp Claimable (through our training partner). Once we confirm your preferred track and dates, we help prepare the course outline and documentation needed for your grant application.
Can we customise the syllabus for our team's daily tools?
Yes. Before each session we review your team's current workflow — whether that is monthly Excel reconciliations, Power BI dashboards, or Microsoft 365 Copilot / ChatGPT / Gemini usage — and tailor exercises accordingly.
Do participants need technical or coding backgrounds?
Not for foundational tracks. We separate beginner, intermediate, and advanced cohorts so non-technical staff build confidence without getting lost, while experienced analysts still learn faster methods.
